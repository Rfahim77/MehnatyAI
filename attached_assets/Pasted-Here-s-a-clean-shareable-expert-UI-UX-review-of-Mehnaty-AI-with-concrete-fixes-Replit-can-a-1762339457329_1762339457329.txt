3) Make the landing page discoverable (SSR/prerender)

Right now, a simple fetch of the root returns no static content (client-only render). That means weak SEO, empty link previews, and slower first paint. Fix: prerender the landing (or SSR minimal HTML).  ￼

Minimal prerender path
	•	Build a static index.html with your hero, features, and footer baked in.
	•	Add proper meta tags (below).
	•	Serve it as the default route, and let the SPA hydrate after.

Meta to add in <head>

<title>Mehnaty AI | مهنتي</title>
<meta name="description" content="Mehnaty AI | مدرّب مهني يعتمد على الذكاء الاصطناعي: مراجعة السيرة، تخصيص للأدوار، خطة مهارات ٣–٦ أشهر، وتجهيز للمقابلات—بالعربية.">
